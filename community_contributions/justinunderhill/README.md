# Accountable MVP: justinunderhill

A single-user sales CRM that runs locally: a dashboard with revenue and deals-won charts, a sales funnel and a "Your day" column of follow-ups, plus organizations, contacts, deals, activities with follow-ups, and a drag-and-drop pipeline board. FastAPI, SQLAlchemy Core and SQLite on the back end, React with TanStack Table, Recharts and dnd-kit on the front end. One `npm test` runs pytest, Vitest and Playwright in Chrome.

These are the documents I wrote on Week 1 Day 3: `intent.md`, `spec.md`, `plan.md` and `REVIEW.md`.

The build is in my fork: https://github.com/justinunderhill/accountable
